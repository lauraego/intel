## Why do I sabotage relationships when they get serious?

Same trigger. Different room.

The relationship moves from exciting to real. They say they love you. They want you to meet their family. They start making plans with your name in them. And something in your chest braces.

Then you find the flaw. You get busy. You start the fight you can feel coming before it exists. You test them until they fail.
