In common numerology, your name means one number. Each letter is given a value, the values are added, and the total is reduced to a single digit called your Expression or Destiny number. ## What is a name number in numerology?

A name number is the most common calculation on any numerology site. You type your name. Every letter is matched to a number from 1 to 9. The numbers are added, then reduced again until one digit is left. Some calculators stop early at 11, 22 or 33 and call those master numbers.

That digit gets a paragraph. A 1 is a leader. A 7 is a seeker. A 3 is a communicator.

Same paragraph for everyone who lands on the same digit. Enormous numbers of people share your name number. Not one of them shares your name, your birth or your Scroll.

You read the paragraph and something in your chest stays flat. It's close. It's flattering. It describes someone. It doesn't describe you.

That flatness is information. Your body knows the difference between a label and a recognition.

Because they don't agree on the rules.

One site uses your full birth name. Another uses whatever you typed, nickname and all. One keeps 11 and 22 as master numbers, another reduces them to 2 and 4. One counts Y as a vowel, another doesn't. Every one of those choices moves the total.

So you end up with three numbers, three paragraphs and no idea which one is you.
